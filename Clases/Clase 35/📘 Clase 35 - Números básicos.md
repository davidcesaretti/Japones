

### Del 1 al 10

- **ichi** = 1
- **ni** = 2
- **san** = 3
- **yon / shi** = 4
- **go** = 5
- **roku** = 6
- **nana / shichi** = 7
- **hachi** = 8
- **kyuu / ku** = 9
- **juu** = 10

---

## Decenas, centenas y miles

### 10, 100, 1000

- **juu** (十) = 10
- **hyaku** (百) = 100
- **sen** (千) = 1000

### Ejemplos

- **juu en** = 10 yenes
- **hyaku en** = 100 yenes
- **sen en** = 1000 yenes
- **gosen en** = 5000 yenes
